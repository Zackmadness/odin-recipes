English Muffin Pizzas

Description

English Muffin Pizzas are a quick and easy meal that delivers all the classic flavors of pizza in a fun, personal-sized serving.

They are perfect for busy weeknights, after-school snacks, or letting kids customize their own toppings. With just a few ingredients, dinner can be ready in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 4 English muffins, split in half
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 16 slices pepperoni

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Arrange the English muffin halves on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread pizza sauce evenly across each half.
  4. Top with mozzarella cheese.
  5. Add pepperoni or your favorite toppings.
  6. Bake for 10 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and lightly browned.
  7. Allow to cool slightly before serving.
