Biscuits and Gravy

Description

Classic Southern biscuits and gravy pairs fluffy homemade or refrigerated biscuits with a rich, savory breakfast sausage gravy. A staple breakfeast food that is good both homemade and to dine out for.

This biscuits and gravy recipe uses jumbo buttermilk biscuits and pork sausage crumbles for a hearty, family-favorite breakfast that's ready in just 15 minutes.

ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) can of biscuits
  • 1 (9.6 ounce) package of pork sausage
  • 1/4 cup flourAll-purpose flour
  • 2 1/2Milk
  • Salt and Pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Arrange biscuits 1 to 2 inches apart on an ungreased cookie shee then bake in the preheated oven until golden brown, about 13 to 15 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, cook sausage in a large skillet over medium heat until thoroughly heated, stirring frequently, about 5 to 6 minutes.
  4. Stir in flour until well combined
  5. Gradually add milk, stirring continuously, until the gravy thickens and comes to a boil. Reduce heat to medium-low; simmer and stir for 2 more minutes. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  6. Put biscuits on plate and top with gravey. Enjoy!
